# Harvest timing and pod quality
Topic: harvest

## When pods are ready
An okra flower opens for one day, and its pod reaches picking size about 4–6 days later, sooner in warm weather (the app counts 5 days, or 4 when the mean air temperature is 27 °C or more). The camera counts open flowers each day, so today's flowers become the pods to pick in about five days.

## Picking size and grades
Pick okra pods at 7–10 cm long for grade A, the best fresh-market quality. Pods of 10–12 cm are grade B. Pods of 12 cm or more are overgrown: tough and fibrous, not good for fresh sale, and sold to processors (pickles, powder) instead. Pods under 7 cm are too small; leave them for another day.

## How often to pick
Pick okra every day, or at least every other day, in the peak season. A pod passes from ready to overgrown in one or two days, which is why the app marks "must pick today" pods. Leaving overgrown pods on the plant also slows the plant's production of new pods, so remove them even if they go to processors.

## How to pick
Pick in the cool early morning, before about 11:00, when pods are crisp. Cut or snap the stem just above the cap, wear gloves (okra plants can irritate skin), and handle pods gently: bruised okra turns black within hours. Work through the rows marked "must pick" first, then the ready rows.

## Before heavy rain
If heavy rain is forecast, pick the outdoor fields first and early. Wet pods spoil faster, and slopes or flooded paths may be unsafe to work on afterwards.
