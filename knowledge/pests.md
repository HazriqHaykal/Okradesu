# Common okra pests
Topic: pests

## Aphids
Aphids are small green or black insects clustered on the young shoots and leaf undersides of okra. They weaken plants and leave sticky honeydew where sooty mould grows. Check shoot tips weekly, remove heavily infested shoots, and encourage natural enemies such as ladybirds.

## Whiteflies
Whiteflies are tiny white flying insects under okra leaves that rise in a cloud when disturbed. Besides weakening plants, they spread okra leaf curl and yellow vein viruses. Use yellow sticky traps to monitor them, insect-proof netting on indoor rooms, and weed control around the fields.

## Spider mites
Spider mites cause fine pale speckling and fine webbing on okra leaves, and increase in hot, dry conditions (humidity below about 40%). Keep humidity in the target range and check leaf undersides with a hand lens during dry spells.

## Stink bugs and pod borers
Stink bugs and caterpillars damage okra flowers and pods, leaving marks or holes that make pods unsellable as fresh grade A. Check pods while picking and remove damaged ones.

## When to get help
Use monitoring, netting, traps and plant hygiene first. If a pest keeps building up, ask JA or the agricultural extension office which registered control measures are allowed for okra, and the pre-harvest interval.
