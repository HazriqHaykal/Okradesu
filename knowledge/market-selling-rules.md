# Selling okra: grades, prices and surplus
Topic: market

## Prices by grade
The co-op's farm-gate prices for okra are grade A (7–10 cm pods) ¥1,000 per kg, grade B (10–12 cm) ¥700 per kg, and overgrown pods (12 cm and over) ¥300 per kg to processors such as pickle makers.

## Surplus alerts
Surplus is the forecast okra harvest minus the kilos already reserved by buyers for that day. More than 10% unsold is an amber alert; more than 25% is a red alert. Because okra keeps only 1–2 days at room temperature, surplus should be listed at least one or two days ahead, so buyers can reserve it in time.

## Surplus listings
A surplus listing offers grade A okra at 20% off (¥800 per kg) to nearby restaurants, wholesalers and processors. List the biggest red alerts first. Once a buyer reserves, confirm the reservation so the buyer can plan the pickup.

## Overgrown pods
Okra pods that will be overgrown by tomorrow should be picked today anyway and offered to processors rather than thrown away: it saves the plant's energy and earns something from pods that cannot sell fresh.

## Year-round supply
The outdoor fields supply okra in summer (about June to October); the indoor rooms supply the off-season (about October to June), when prices are usually higher. June and October overlap, which smooths the hand-over for regular buyers.
