# Storage and shelf life after picking
Topic: storage

## How long okra keeps
Fresh okra is very perishable. At room temperature (about 25 °C) it keeps good quality for only about 1–2 days. Stored at 7–10 °C with high humidity (90–95%), it keeps about 7–10 days.

## Cold storage
Store okra at 7–10 °C, not colder: below about 7 °C okra suffers chilling injury, with pitting, dull colour and faster rotting once it warms up. Keep humidity high, for example in perforated bags, so pods do not shrivel.

## Handling
Do not wash okra before storage; moisture on the pods speeds decay. Avoid stacking pods deeply or bruising them, since bruises blacken. Keep picked batches shaded and cool while they wait for pickup.

## First in, first out
Sell or deliver the oldest okra batch first. The app marks batches older than 24 hours as "Deliver first". Because okra keeps so briefly, surplus should be listed at least one or two days before its harvest date, so buyers can reserve it before quality drops.
