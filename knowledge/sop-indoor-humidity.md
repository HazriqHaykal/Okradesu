# SOP: high humidity in an indoor room
Topic: sop

## Why it matters
The indoor rooms (the school classroom and gymnasium, the vacant house, the old post office) have little natural airflow. Humidity above 75% for several hours, especially overnight, lets mildew and other fungal diseases spread before any spots are visible on the okra.

## What to do
Run the fans: the automatic rule runs them 20 minutes every hour while humidity is above 75%. If humidity stays high, run them continuously for a few hours and check that the vents are open. Water in the morning rather than the evening, and at the base of the plants.

## Check the room
Look for a fan that is not spinning, blocked vents, standing water on the floor, and crowded plants that need pruning. Compare the light check: a row with much weaker LED light often has weaker, more diseased plants.

## Follow up
Watch the overnight humidity trend in the 24-hour chart for the next few days, and check the leaf undersides in the most humid rows for early mildew.
