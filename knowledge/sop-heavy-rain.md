# SOP: heavy rain, flood and landslide risk
Topic: sop

## When this applies
The app treats 50 mm or more of rain in a day as heavy rain. The riverside outdoor field (Field A) is at risk of flooding; the sloped outdoor field (Field B, on the terraced slope) is at risk of landslides, especially when its soil is already wet.

## Before the rain
Pick the ready and must-pick pods on the outdoor fields early, before the rain starts. Skip watering the outdoor fields: the outdoor pump rule already skips when rain is forecast. At the riverside field, move tools, crates and picked batches to high ground, and warn neighbours through LINE.

## During the rain
Stay off the sloped field while it rains and until the ground has drained, and do not work below the slope. Do not walk into flooded areas near the river.

## After the rain
Check drainage and standing water, since waterlogged okra roots can rot within days. Look for leaf spot and mildew over the next week, because wet, warm conditions favour them. Check the solar sensor nodes and the gateway for water damage.
